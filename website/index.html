Features

Three effects. Zero setup.

Presentools draws a transparent overlay above everything on screen and follows your cursor at 60 Hz. Your deck never leaves the app you already use.

Spotlight

Dims the whole screen except a soft pool of light under your cursor. Attention goes to one part of the slide — the diagram, the number, the caveat line.

no permission needed

Laser Pointer

A small, high-contrast dot that stays exactly where you left it while the audience looks. It does not fade, drift or scroll away with the slide.

no permission needed

Zoom Lens

Magnifies whatever is under the cursor, so a footnote or a config line is readable from the back row. Follows the mouse in 8 Hz steps to stay cool.

asks for Screen Recording

Shortcuts

Four keys. Always the same.

Every effect is a toggle — press once to turn it on, press again to turn it off. ⌥Esc kills everything, whatever is active, because that is the moment you need it.

⌥0All effects off
⌥1Spotlight
⌥2Laser Pointer
⌥3Zoom Lens
⌥EscAll off — panic key

Open Shortcuts… from the menu bar to rebind any key, or switch an effect to hold or auto-repeat if you prefer that over a toggle.

Install

Drag it in, or brew it.

DMG — recommended

Download the .dmg, open it, drag Presentools.app into /Applications. First launch asks whether to start at login.

# 812 KB, no installer, no admin rights
# then:
open Presentools.dmg

Homebrew

A cask lives in this repo's tap, wired to the release asset and its published SHA-256 digest, so brew upgrade just works.

brew tap ardith666/presentools
brew install --cask presentools
First launch says "unidentified developer"? That is expected. Presentools is ad-hoc signed — there is no paid Apple Developer ID behind it. Right-click (or Control-click) Presentools in Applications and choose Open once. After that it launches normally, forever.
Screen Recording permission is requested on first use of Zoom Lens only. Decline it and Spotlight and Laser Pointer still work perfectly — one degraded feature, not a dead app.

FAQ

The things people ask

Does it work inside PowerPoint or Keynote?

Yes — and that is the point. Presentools is a transparent overlay that sits above whatever is on screen, so it works over any app: presentation software, a PDF in Preview, a browser deck, a video call. Your deck is not imported, converted, or re-rendered.

Why macOS 15.2 and later?

Zoom Lens grabs one rectangle of the screen at native resolution, and the API that does that — captureImage(in:) — arrived in macOS 15.2. Spotlight and Laser Pointer use nothing newer than the original AppKit, so the whole app builds with no availability checks and no compatibility branches.

Will an update break my Screen Recording permission?

No, deliberately. An update downloads the new .dmg, verifies its SHA-256 against the digest in the release notes, and opens the folder — it never replaces the running app in place. Replacing a signed bundle in place can silently reset the permission grant and break Zoom Lens with no obvious cause.

Is it really free, with no catch?

MIT licensed, no paid tier, no account, no telemetry, no network calls except the release check you can turn off in the menu bar. If it stops being useful to you, you already have the source.

Can I turn off the update check?

Yes — uncheck Check for Updates Automatically in the menu bar, or run Check for Updates… on your own schedule.
